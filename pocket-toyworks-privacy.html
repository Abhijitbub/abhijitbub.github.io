Pocket Toyworks Privacy Policy

Updated 15 October 2026 · Version 1.1.0

50-level closed test with Google test ads. No real-money purchases or accounts.

Who we are

Pocket Toyworks is published by JSCA Games. Contact jscagames6@gmail.com for support and privacy questions.

Scope

This policy applies to version 1.1.0, a 50-level friends-and-family closed test with Google sample ads. It has no real-money purchases, player accounts, cloud saves or online leaderboards.

Local game information

Puzzle progress, move history, timers, lives, coins, gifts, tools, completed orders, profile name and character, settings and ad frequency counters are saved on your device. Playtest event logs also stay on the device. The game does not upload this local profile or progress to the developer. Clearing app storage or uninstalling deletes local progress. Android app backup is disabled.

Google test advertisements

The Android game includes Google Mobile Ads Unity SDK 11.5.0, Android Mobile Ads SDK 25.4.0 and User Messaging Platform 4.0.0. Only Google sample app and ad unit identifiers are configured; these are test advertisements and do not generate advertising revenue for us. Ads require Internet access. Puzzles remain playable without a connection or when ads are unavailable.

Information processed by Google

Advertising SDKs may collect and share IP address and approximate location inferred from it, ad and app interactions, performance and diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as app set ID for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Test mode does not make network requests anonymous. Android advertising-ID and AdServices permissions are removed in this release. The game does not request precise location, contacts, camera or microphone access. Google states that SDK data is encrypted in transit. See Google Privacy Policy and Mobile Ads data disclosures.

Choices and age treatment

This closed test uses restricted, non-personalized ad requests with child-directed and under-age treatment and a G content rating. These flags apply conservatively to every player and are not a declaration of anyone's actual age. The User Messaging Platform checks privacy status; ads are requested only when it permits requests. Where a privacy options form is required, Settings then Privacy provides Ad privacy choices. Ads are optional for life rewards, and unavailable ads do not charge coins or lives. You can play without networking. Target audience declarations and any future live-ad configuration require separate review.

Ad placements

An optional rewarded ad can restore one life, up to eight completed rewards per UTC day. Capped interstitials may appear after a completed main level once onboarding and active-play thresholds are met. There are no banner ads or ads during a puzzle, tutorial, restart or timeout. Ad playback pauses the puzzle clock and mutes game music.

Support and voluntary feedback

If you email jscagames6@gmail.com, we receive the address, message and attachments you provide. We use them to respond and investigate reported issues; please avoid unnecessary sensitive information. Contact the same address to request deletion of support messages, subject to legal retention requirements. Google Play test feedback and platform diagnostics are handled under Google's own services and settings.

Retention and third parties

The developer does not maintain a server copy of your game progress. Google manages SDK data and retention under its policies and applicable choices. We do not sell local player profiles. There are no separate developer analytics, account or purchase SDKs. Optional haptics use the vibration permission.

Changes

We will update this policy when the game's data practices change, including before switching to live advertising or adding online services.
