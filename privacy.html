Cargo Escape
Privacy policy

Updated: 7 October 2026

Version 1.0.0 (code 1) is the offline release and does not activate advertising. Starting with version 1.1.1 (code 3), eligible adult players may receive advertising as described below. These advertising practices apply only when using an ad-enabled version.

Who we are

Cargo Escape is published by JSCA Games. Contact jscagames6@gmail.com for support or privacy requests.

Local game information

Puzzle progress, coins, depot upgrades, lives and their regeneration timestamps, selected cosmetic crew, optional local display name, and audio and vibration preferences are stored on your device. The game has no account login, cloud saves, in-app purchases or separate gameplay analytics service. The optional dispatcher display name is a local nickname only and is not transmitted by our game. We do not ask for your email, precise location or date of birth inside the game.

Age choice

The game is intended for ages 13 and older. Ad-enabled versions ask for an age group: under 13, 13–17, 18 or older, or prefer not to say. This choice stays on the device and can be changed in Settings. The game does not start Google consent or advertising requests for players who select under 18 or prefer not to say. All puzzles remain playable without ads. Changing from an adult choice stops further game-initiated ad requests; it does not erase information already processed by Google.

Advertising and privacy choices

For players selecting 18 or older, the game uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform to determine applicable privacy choices before requesting ads. Where required, a Google privacy form appears. Settings provides Privacy Choices when Google requires that option. If privacy checks fail, gameplay continues without ads.

Advertising includes occasional interstitial ads between levels and an optional video for 50 extra coins on level completion or one additional life when lives are not full. A reward is granted only after the advertising provider confirms it was earned. Ad availability is not guaranteed. The game requests non-personalized ads and restricted data processing, disables Google's publisher first-party ID and removes advertising-ID and Android AdServices permissions. Non-personalized advertising still processes information to deliver, measure and secure ads.

Information processed by Google

When adult advertising or consent services are active, Google may process IP address (including approximate location), device and app identifiers, app/ad interactions, diagnostic and performance information, and consent choices. We do not request precise location. Advertising-ID permission is removed, but this does not mean all device identifiers are removed. These services use information for advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, security and compliance. Data is encrypted in transit.

Google may process information on servers outside your country. Its retention periods and processing depend on its services and policies; JSCA Games does not operate those systems. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.

Support and website

If you email us, we receive your email address and the content you send, and use it to respond and resolve problems. Our email provider processes correspondence. Do not send passwords or payment credentials. Opening this policy uses your browser; GitHub Pages may process technical request information including IP address under the GitHub privacy statement. Google Play processes store activity under its own policies.

Retention, deletion and your rights

Local saves and age preferences remain until you clear Android Settings → Apps → Cargo Escape → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall, subject to device behavior. Clearing storage resets progress. Automatic Android app backup is disabled; manufacturers may provide separate transfer features. Clearing local storage does not delete data already processed by Google.

We retain support correspondence as necessary to handle requests and applicable obligations. Email us to request access, correction or deletion of information you provided. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to object, restrict processing, withdraw consent or complain to a supervisory authority. Use in-game Privacy Choices where available for Google's consent settings and Google's privacy controls for Google-held data. We may need to verify your request.

Children and security

The game is not directed at children under 13. Parents or guardians can contact us about information submitted by a child. It does not access the camera, microphone or contacts. Vibration is optional. No storage or communication system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Updates

We will update this policy when data practices change and provide additional notice or consent where required. The version-specific scope above preserves the description of the earlier offline release.
