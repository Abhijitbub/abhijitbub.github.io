Cargo Escape
Privacy policy

Effective date: 16 September 2026

This policy applies to Cargo Escape version 1.0.0 (version code 1), the offline test release published by JSCA Games for Android. Advertising is disabled in this version.

Who we are

JSCA Games publishes Cargo Escape. For privacy questions or support, email jscagames6@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The game stores your puzzle progress, coins, depot upgrades and sound, music and vibration preferences locally on your device. It does not require an account or ask for your name, email address, date of birth or location.

Information collected or shared by the game

This offline version does not send personal information, gameplay activity or device identifiers to JSCA Games or advertising providers. It has no internet permission, analytics reporting, cloud saves, in-app purchases or active advertising. Advertising and consent libraries may be included in the installation, but their advertising initialization is disabled in this release and the app cannot make internet requests.

Vibration is used only for optional gameplay feedback. The game does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or precise location.

Support messages and external links

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond to your request, troubleshoot issues and maintain necessary support records. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information. Our email provider processes these messages to deliver the service.

Opening this policy from the game launches your browser. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose provider may process technical request information such as your IP address and browser details. See the GitHub privacy statement. Email services and Google Play also process information under their own policies when you use those services; that activity is separate from this game's offline operation.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until you clear the app's storage or remove the app, subject to your device's behavior. To delete it, use Android Settings → Apps → Cargo Escape → Storage → Clear storage. This also resets your progress. Automatic Android app backup is disabled in this version; device manufacturers may provide separate transfer or backup features.

We retain support correspondence only as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations. You can email us to request access, correction or deletion of information you sent us. We may need to verify that the request comes from the person concerned. Applicable privacy rights depend on where you live.

Age audience

Cargo Escape is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. This offline release does not collect age information or serve ads to any age group. A parent or guardian who believes personal information has been sent to us can contact us using the email above to request its removal.

Security

Game saves use Android's app storage. Protect your device and email account against unauthorized access. No storage or communication method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes to this policy

If a future version introduces advertising or changes how information is processed, we will update this policy and provide any required in-app notice and privacy choices before enabling that behavior. This policy does not describe advertising as active in the current offline release.
