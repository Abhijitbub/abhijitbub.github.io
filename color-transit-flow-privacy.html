JSCA GAMES / PRIVACY

Color Transit Flow

Privacy policy for the Android game, package com.jscagames.colortransitflow. Last updated October 15, 2026, covering the ads-enabled version 1.2.0 and the earlier offline edition.

All routes can be played with ads off. Adults can choose to enable Google AdMob advertising; players under 18 use the ads-off option. No account or in-app purchase is required.

Who we are

Color Transit Flow is provided by JSCA Games. Contact jscagames6@gmail.com for support or privacy questions.

Game information on your device

Your puzzle, undo history, route progress, earned virtual coins, lives and regeneration time, conductor, cosmetic frame, local captain name, station theme and audio/vibration preferences are stored on your device. The captain name is a local display name and is not sent to JSCA Games or used to identify an account. JSCA Games does not operate a game-progress server or collect these records. Ads participation and Google privacy choices are also saved locally.

Optional advertising

Version 1.2.0 can show optional rewarded videos for virtual coins and occasional interstitial ads between routes when an adult enables advertising. The game requests updated privacy information through Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting ads, displays consent messages where required and provides applicable privacy options in Settings → Ad privacy. Advertising does not initialize on the ads-off path. The earlier version 1.0.0 has no active advertising or network permission.

When enabled, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID, app/ad interactions such as launches, taps and video views, and diagnostic/performance information. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. SDK data is encrypted in transit. The developer has no separate in-game analytics service. See Google's privacy policy and Google's advertising SDK disclosures.

Your controls

You can keep ads off, change advertising participation in Settings, and use the privacy choices button where Google requires it. Choosing ads off stops new ad requests; it does not erase data already processed by Google. Android settings provide advertising-ID reset/deletion controls. Network errors or unavailable ads do not prevent local gameplay. This policy does not promise personalized advertising or that refusing consent removes every category of SDK processing; applicable consent options and limited-ad behavior are managed by Google.

Consent and regional privacy choices

When you choose to enable advertising, Google's User Messaging Platform contacts Google to check which privacy choices apply. This consent-service request may involve connection and device information, such as your IP address, even before an ad is requested. The game waits for a successful privacy check and Google's permission to request ads before initializing advertising. Google privacy messages and the options available depend on your location and the applicable message configuration.

For eligible users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Google message provides consent choices and information about advertising partners and purposes. You can revisit available choices through Settings → Ad privacy & optional rewards → Change privacy choices.

For eligible users in supported US states, Google's privacy options provide applicable choices about the sale or sharing of personal information and targeted advertising, including a “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” option where available. Use the same in-game privacy choices entry point to review or change those options. You can also choose Keep ads off / Under 18 to stop new ad requests in the game.

Storage and deletion

Local game data stays until you clear app storage or uninstall. Android game-data backup is disabled. Clearing storage removes your local progress, coins, lives records and preferences; JSCA Games has no server copy to restore. For data already processed by Google, consult Google's privacy controls and retention policy. For support correspondence held by JSCA Games, email us to request deletion.

Device features and external links

Optional vibration, music and sound effects run locally. Policy/support links open your browser, whose provider and website host may process connection information. If you email support, we use your email address and the details you provide to respond and retain correspondence only as needed for the request and related issues. Avoid sending passwords or payment details.

Audience

The intended audience is ages 13 and older. We do not request a birth date. Players under 18 should select Keep ads off / Under 18. Cosmetic characters, frames and virtual coins have no real-money value and there are no purchases.

Changes

We update this policy and its effective date when data practices change. Store disclosures and in-app privacy information will reflect material changes.
